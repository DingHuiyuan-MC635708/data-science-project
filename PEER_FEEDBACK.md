## Classmate feedback

The reviewer understood the project as an investigation of the relationship between monthly visitor arrivals and hotel occupancy in Macao from 2015 to 2025. They identified the main finding as a strong positive association (Pearson r = 0.947), with a large disruption during 2020–2022 followed by recovery.

The reviewer recognized that correlation does not establish causation, and that seasonality, hotel supply, public policy, and the exceptional COVID-19 period may affect the relationship. They recommended making limitations more prominent, comparing subperiods, adding a data dictionary, and clarifying the reproduction instructions.

## Improvement implemented

- Separated **Limitations** and **Next improvements** into prominent sections in both the portfolio and project README.
- Clarified that uncontrolled factors and aggregate data limit interpretation of individual behaviour.
- Added `data/README.md` to explain the variables, units, source indicators, date coverage, and handling of revisions.
- Added explicit download, environment setup, execution, and expected-output steps to the project README; linked them and the data dictionary from the website.
- Retained the **existing** 2015–2019, 2020–2022, and 2023–2025 correlation comparison. It was present before the feedback and is not falsely described as newly added.
